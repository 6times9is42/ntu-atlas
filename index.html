    For freshmen

    Day 1 essentials

    New to NTU?
    Start here.

    Your first few weeks involve a lot of portals you've never heard of. These are the eight things worth getting done before classes start — in roughly the right order.

    Register your modules
    STARS opens in waves — log in early and have backup modules ready.
    Check NTULearn
    Professors often post pre-reading and syllabus before Week 1.
    Sort housing early
    Halls fill up fast. Apply through the Hall Portal as soon as applications open.
    Explore financial aid
    Bursary deadlines are often missed by freshmen who don't know they qualify.
    Read your degree audit
    Understand graduation requirements from Day 1 to avoid scrambling in Year 4.
    Set up CareerAxis
    Even in Year 1 — internship positions close months before the start date.
    Bookmark library booking
    Good rooms fill by 8 AM during exam season. Bookmark and book early.
    Join the community
    The NTU Atlas Telegram has seniors ready to answer questions you're embarrassed to Google.
